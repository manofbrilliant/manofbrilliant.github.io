ABOUT THE COMPANY

AIcyborg Corporation

AIcyborg Corporation was founded by Yadlapalli Phanindra in 2022 with a simple but ambitious vision: to reduce the complexity created by having too many applications and bring multiple functionalities together into simpler, more powerful, and easy-to-use experiences.

The company initially started in online mode in 2022, working in collaboration with Bluelearn and other student communities to explore ideas, build projects, and develop solutions with a community-driven approach. These early collaborations helped establish the foundation for AIcyborg Corporation and provided valuable experience in understanding how students and users interact with technology.

After moving forward independently, AIcyborg Corporation developed its first major client project in 2024, called ManofBrilliant. Built during the rapidly emerging era of AI, ManofBrilliant was designed primarily for school and student-oriented purposes. It is an interactive AI-based model where students can interact with a chatbot according to different characters, roles, or fields, creating a more engaging and personalized learning experience.

The ManofBrilliant project was developed specifically for a client and was delivered in December 2024. It represented an important milestone for AIcyborg Corporation, demonstrating the company's ability to transform emerging AI technologies into practical applications for real-world users.

AIcyborg Corporation has also established an online presence through platforms such as Crunchbase, where the company has achieved a position within approximately the top 10,000 companies, competing within a global ecosystem that includes companies from around the world.
